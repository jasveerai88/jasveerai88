# Hi, I'm Jasveer

I build free, local-first tools for faceless and AI video creators — from measured failure data on real channels, not opinions.

## Featured
**[Faceless QC Lite](https://github.com/jasveerai88/faceless-qc-lite)** — free pre-upload checker for faceless videos: loudness, dead air, black frames, caption speed, resolution. Runs 100% on your machine. MIT.

Real catch: it flagged one of my own videos at −21.6 LUFS (target −14) before upload.

**[Payout Tracker](https://github.com/jasveerai88/payout-tracker)** — a balance is not money: REALIZED vs PAYABLE vs STUCK payouts, fees and FX in CAD + INR.

**[Demand Miner](https://github.com/jasveerai88/demand-miner)** — finds buyers hiding in public posts, offline, sellers filtered out.

**[Caption Toolkit](https://github.com/jasveerai88/caption-toolkit)** — caption speed check + fix + SRT↔VTT convert.

**[Shorts Packager](https://github.com/jasveerai88/shorts-packager)** — Shorts title fitter for the 100-char rule — hashtags that fit stay in the title, the rest go to the description.

**[Thumbnail Checker](https://github.com/jasveerai88/thumbnail-checker)** — pre-upload thumbnail resolution, aspect ratio, and filename checks.

**[Retention Checker](https://github.com/jasveerai88/retention-checker)** — pre-publish retention check for faceless & AI video: hook window, dead zones, caption pacing, loudness, platform spec. Refuses to pass a video it can't measure.

**[Shorts Caption Studio](https://github.com/jasveerai88/shorts-caption-studio)** — animated word-highlight captions for Shorts, locally: the subscription-tool look without the subscription. Word timing honestly labelled as estimated.

**[Seller Profit Truth](https://github.com/jasveerai88/seller-profit-truth)** — true per-listing profit for Etsy & Gumroad sellers: every fee line separately, loss-makers flagged, break-even prices that survive the fees.

**[GST/HST Tracker](https://github.com/jasveerai88/gst-hst-tracker)** — GST/HST tracking for Canadian freelancers & small business: net tax owing per quarter, ITCs, and the $30,000 small-supplier alarm before it surprises you. A prep aid, not tax advice.

**[Quote Follow-Up](https://github.com/jasveerai88/quote-follow-up)** — never lose a quote to silence: a tiny local board that flags follow-ups due at 3, 7 and 14 days and writes the message for you.

**[Creator Dedupe](https://github.com/jasveerai88/creator-dedupe)** — stop repeating your own topics: checks a new video idea against everything you've published and flags duplicates before you produce them.

**[Brand-Deal Pipeline](https://github.com/jasveerai88/brand-deal-pipeline)** — brand deals from pitch to actually-paid: deliverable deadlines, forgotten invoices, and unpaid-invoice chasing on one tiny local board.
**[Clip Miner](https://github.com/jasveerai88/clip-miner)** — your next Short is already inside your long video: ranks the most clip-worthy moments from the transcript and cuts them for you.

**[Booking Board](https://github.com/jasveerai88/booking-board)** — a tiny local appointment board for small clinics, salons & trades: double-bookings refused, free slots shown, reminders written for you.
